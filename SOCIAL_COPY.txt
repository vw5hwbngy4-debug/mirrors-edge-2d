Title:
Mirror’s Edge 2D Running on iPhone Browser + Catalyst Companion Map Revival

Description:
Mirror’s Edge 2D is running in a modern iPhone browser again, with touch controls.

This preservation build uses the known offline Flash game and Ruffle, with a mobile control layer added around the original game. I’ve now integrated it with City of Glass 3D, my browser-native reconstruction of the discontinued Mirror’s Edge Catalyst Companion App map experience from recovered companion-map data.

Play Mirror’s Edge 2D:
https://vw5hwbngy4-debug.github.io/mirrors-edge-2d/

Explore the Catalyst companion map reconstruction:
https://vw5hwbngy4-debug.github.io/mirrors-edge-2d/catalyst/

Project provenance / creator:
https://vw5hwbngy4-debug.github.io/mirrors-edge-2d/about/

Built and preserved by Raynor Eissens, 2026.
To my knowledge, the Catalyst project is the first publicly documented browser-native reconstruction of the discontinued companion app’s 3D City of Glass map experience. This is a narrow claim about the map experience, not a recreation of EA’s retired backend or social services.

Independent fan preservation/reconstruction. Mirror’s Edge and related properties belong to Electronic Arts and DICE. No affiliation is claimed.
