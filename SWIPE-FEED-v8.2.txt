SpawnMap Swipe v8.2

Landing stability fix:
- The server-status landing is now shown only once per browser tab/session.
- Dismissing it writes sessionStorage before the exit animation begins.
- If Safari/Chrome refreshes because of an aggressive vertical gesture, the landing does not return.
- An inline boot guard prevents a visible landing flash on reload.
- html/body are fixed and overscroll is suppressed to reduce mobile pull-to-refresh / rubber-band behavior.
- No visual redesign and no viewer/map changes from v8.1.
