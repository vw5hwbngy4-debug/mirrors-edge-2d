MADE FROM CHAT — MIRROR'S EDGE CATALYST / CITY OF GLASS 3D
MOBILE + DESKTOP RESPONSIVE PASS V2

WHAT THIS EXPORT IS
A cleaned standalone presentation of the 3D City of Glass reconstruction from the supplied GlassGallery project. It is intended to be embedded or linked from Made from Chat as a project artifact.

KEPT
- Complete 48-layer GLB City of Glass model
- The Shard sightseeing point
- Birdman sightseeing point + saved meditation view
- Faith Fragment environmental point
- Elysium Ascent runner challenge + three route images
- Saved camera viewpoints from the supplied project
- Free 3D orbit / zoom / pan controls
- Fan-project disclaimer

MOBILE / RESPONSIVE PASS
- Sightseeing points are collapsible on both desktop and smartphone
- Smartphone starts with the sightseeing drawer closed so the city owns the screen
- Smartphone sightseeing uses a vertical bottom drawer, never a horizontal card strip
- No body-level horizontal scrolling or out-of-bounds sightseeing cards
- Safe-area spacing for modern iPhone screens
- Portrait and landscape layouts are handled separately
- Overview camera compensates for narrow portrait aspect ratios and fits the preserved city core + sightseeing points
- Touch controls stay directly on the 3D canvas: one finger rotates, pinch zooms, two fingers pan
- Mobile rendering keeps all 48 geometry layers but reduces GPU load with a lower render pixel ratio, no heavy edge-geometry overlay, and shared materials
- Desktop retains the detailed edge treatment and can keep the sightseeing panel open or collapse it

REMOVED
- DeeplyLLM Gallery and deeplyllm-room
- GPT vibecoded tools / portfolio entries
- Cold Waves audio and personal art layer
- Screenshot placement and localStorage pin creation
- Developer coordinate tools
- Five Human Areas navigation
- GlassGallery-specific gallery/about routing

INTEGRATION
Serve this directory over HTTP/HTTPS and open index.html. The page loads Three.js 0.160.0 from unpkg.com and loads all 48 local GLB files from assets/glb/.

POSITIONING COPY
"I rebuilt the city map from the Mirror's Edge Catalyst companion app together with GPT. Here you can visit the City of Glass again in the browser, but this time the map is not locked to a bird's-eye view: you can explore the reconstructed city freely in 3D from every angle."
