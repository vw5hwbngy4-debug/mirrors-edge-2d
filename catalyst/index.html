Browser preservation · Raynor Eissens · 2026

The Catalyst companion map, reopened in 3D.

City of Glass 3D is Raynor Eissens' browser-native reconstruction of the discontinued Mirror's Edge Catalyst Companion App map experience, rebuilt from recovered companion-map data and turned into a freely navigable 3D space.

The original companion app offered a fully rendered 3D map alongside game progress, social and profile features. This independent reconstruction focuses on the map itself: the City of Glass geometry, landmarks, saved views and preserved sightseeing material. It is not a replacement for EA's former online services.

You can orbit the complete city, zoom between districts and jump to preserved locations including The Shard, Birdman, Faith Fragment and Elysium Ascent.

First-mover scope: to our knowledge, and based on public web searches as of September 15, 2026, this is the first publicly documented browser-native reconstruction of the discontinued Catalyst companion app's 3D City of Glass map experience. This is deliberately narrower than claiming to recreate the full original companion app or its retired EA backend.
