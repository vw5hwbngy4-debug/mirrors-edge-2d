SpawnMap Inspector v0.3

Mobile-first architecture:
- Main page is a scalable compact map-row library. No repeated map detail/viewer content.
- Tapping a row opens a dedicated full-screen map detail surface.
- Detail surface shows a centered 3D Overview first.
- Spectator launches as its own full-screen player-height surface.
- Spectator mobile controls: left stick = camera-relative movement, swipe/drag world = look, Jump = jump.
- CHANGE THIS AREA opens a location-aware iteration prompt that can be copied back to GPT.
- Tactical Ops runtime remains the final authority for UE1 skybox/FakeBackdrop, lighting, pathing and gameplay.
